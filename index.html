Drunk People Group – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy covers the Drunk People Group app for Android and Windows. It explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name or Tipsy name, and an account ID. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Profile picture: if you choose to add one.
  • Messages and pings: messages and pings you send to your drinking buddies.
  • Things you save or post: saved drinks, ratings, home bar plans, pub crawls, friend requests and "I'm at this bar" check-ins.
  • Location: only when you use Nearby Bars, pub crawls or check-ins, and only with your permission. It is used to find bars near you and is not stored. A check-in saves the bar's name, not your phone's location.
  • A device code: a random code for each phone, so the app can alert you about new messages.

We do not collect payment details, contacts, health data or advertising IDs, and the app has no ads.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and keep what you save.
  • To make the social features work: messages, pings, buddies and check-ins.
  • To find nearby bars and plan pub crawls.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Your Tipsy name and profile picture can be seen by other app users.
  • Messages and pings can be seen only by you and the person you sent them to.
  • Check-ins ("I'm at this bar") can be seen by other app users until they expire.
  • Saved drinks, plans and pub crawls are private to you.

Services we use

These companies process data for us only to provide the feature you are using:

  • Supabase – stores accounts and app data, and handles sign-in.
  • Google – Google sign-in (if you use it) and Google Maps for finding bars.

All data is sent over encrypted connections.

How to delete your account and data

  1. Open the Drunk People Group app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your account and all of its data straight away: your profile and profile picture, messages, pings, friend requests, check-ins, ratings, saved drinks and bars, home bar plans and pub crawls.

If you can't use the app, email support.drunkpeoplegroup@gmail.com from the email address on your account and we will delete everything within 30 days.

To delete only some data, remove individual saved drinks, plans or pub crawls inside the app at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists, and delete it as described above when you ask. Check-ins expire on their own after five hours.

Age

The app is only for people of legal drinking age where they live. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about your data: support.drunkpeoplegroup@gmail.com
